- Retrieve full stored content from a previous `fetch_content` call
- Select by `responseId`, optionally narrowing by `url` or `urlIndex`
- Use this when `fetch_content` response summaries are not enough and you need the full extracted text

Usage notes:
  - `responseId` is required
  - At most one selector may be used: `url` or `urlIndex`
  - Retrieval is session-scoped: only content stored in the current session is available
